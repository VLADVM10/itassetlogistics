1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is handled when you visit the IT Asset Logistics website or contact us about equipment storage and logistics services in Ukraine.

It covers website use and business enquiries. Information relating to equipment handled under a separate service agreement may be subject to additional contractual arrangements.

2. Information we collect

When you send an enquiry, we may receive your name, company name, email address, optional telephone number and the information you include in your message.

The website or its hosting services may also process limited technical information, such as an IP address, browser information, access time and security logs, to operate and protect the website.

Please do not include passwords, identification documents, confidential data or information stored on devices in a website enquiry.

3. Why we use this information

We use the information to respond to enquiries, discuss possible services and commercial terms, communicate about your request, maintain website security, understand how the website is used (only with your consent) and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Where the GDPR applies, processing may be based, as appropriate, on steps requested before entering into a contract, legitimate interests in handling business enquiries and protecting the website, compliance with legal obligations or consent where required.

4. Sharing information

Information may be accessible to authorised people handling enquiries and to service providers supporting website hosting, business email, technical maintenance, spam prevention or, only with your consent, website analytics (Google). We may also disclose information when required by applicable law.

We do not sell personal information.

5. International processing

The website and related communications may involve technical service providers operating in Ukraine or other countries. Where relevant data protection rules apply to international transfers, appropriate legal safeguards will be used as required.

6. Retention

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to and follow up on your request, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes or meet legal obligations. When it is no longer needed, it is deleted or anonymised, as appropriate.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

The website uses technologies necessary for its operation, security and language preferences. They do not require consent.

With your consent, we use Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager (services provided by Google), to understand how the website is used: for example, which pages are viewed and whether a request was sent. Google Analytics sets cookies such as _ga, which are kept for up to 2 years. They are set only after you choose “Accept analytics” in the cookie banner. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set and Google Tag Manager is not loaded.

You can change your choice at any time with “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings.

8. Your rights

Depending on the law that applies, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability or to object to certain processing. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it.

You may also have the right to submit a complaint to a competent data protection authority.

9. Security

We take reasonable organisational and technical measures to protect information processed through the website. However, no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Contact and updates

For questions about this Policy or requests relating to your personal information, please use the Contact page of this website.

We may update this Policy when our website, service providers or data-processing practices change. The current version will be published on this page.
