What We Handle

Physical storage media, individually accounted for.

We provide physical storage and handling for data-bearing media removed from corporate IT equipment or retained for future use, return or further processing.

Hard Disk Drives (HDD)

Internal hard drives from computers, servers and other corporate equipment.

Solid-State Drives (SSD)

Solid-state storage devices requiring physical storage and documented handling.

Other Data-Bearing Media

External drives, removable storage devices and other media may be accepted by prior arrangement.

Media can be received individually or in packaged batches. Identification and recording procedures are agreed according to the type and quantity of media.

We do not access or process the data stored on these devices as part of the storage service.

Storage & Handling

Documented handling from receipt to release.

We organise the physical storage of data-bearing media according to agreed procedures, maintaining records of received, stored and released items.

  1. 01

    Receiving

    We receive individual media devices or packaged batches, confirm the number of items or packages and record visible packaging conditions.

  2. 02

    Identification

    Media or packages are registered using available serial numbers, labels or agreed identifiers. The level of individual recording is defined with the client.

  3. 03

    Storage

    Accepted media are placed in designated storage locations, with access and handling arrangements agreed in advance.

  4. 04

    Release

    Media are released or prepared for transfer according to authorised client instructions. The handover is documented.

Physical handling only. Our storage service concerns the physical devices. We do not inspect, access, copy or modify the data stored on them.

After Storage

Your media. Your decision.

When storage is no longer required, we arrange the next step according to your instructions.

Return to Owner

We prepare the stored media for collection or coordinate their return to the client.

Authorised Transfer

Media can be released or transferred to an authorised recipient according to agreed instructions, with the handover documented.

Media Destruction

If physical destruction is required, we can coordinate a separate service through REISSWOLF Ukraine.

Destruction arrangements, pricing and supporting documentation are agreed separately.

Storage does not include data erasure or media destruction. These operations require separate authorisation and service arrangements.
