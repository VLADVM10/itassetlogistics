Typical Scenarios

Different needs. One local equipment partner.

Companies use Equipment Hub Ukraine for different operational needs — from managing employee IT devices to maintaining reserve stock and handling demonstration or project equipment.

01

Employee Equipment Lifecycle

The situation

An international company needs to supply laptops and other devices to employees across Ukraine and arrange their return when assignments end.

How we help

We receive and register equipment, store available devices, coordinate deliveries to employees and arrange returns. Inventory records are updated as equipment moves through the process.

02

Reserve & Replacement Stock

The situation

A company or IT service provider needs replacement devices, spare equipment and accessories available in Ukraine without managing its own local stockroom.

How we help

We maintain equipment stock, track quantities and individual assets, prepare requested items and coordinate dispatch. Returned equipment can be received and held for future use.

03

Demonstration & Project Equipment

The situation

An international manufacturer or technology company sends demonstration units, technical devices or project equipment to Ukraine for customer presentations, installations or phased deployment.

How we help

We receive and register the equipment, store it between assignments, prepare individual shipments or complete kits, and coordinate deliveries and returns — without requiring a dedicated warehouse rental.

Your requirements may be different. We can build an equipment handling workflow around your actual operational needs.
