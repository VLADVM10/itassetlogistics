What We Store

From corporate IT to project equipment.

We provide managed storage for corporate equipment and technical stock. Our service covers sealed boxes, individual devices, reserve stock and project batches — with the level of inventory detail agreed for each request.

01

Corporate IT Equipment

Laptops, desktop computers, monitors, networking devices, peripherals and other corporate IT hardware.

02

Reserve & Replacement Stock

Spare devices, replacement equipment, accessories and technical components held for future use or service requests.

03

Demonstration & Exhibition Equipment

Product samples, demonstration units, presentation kits and exhibition equipment stored between events, customer visits or assignments.

04

Measuring instruments, electronic devices, installation kits and technical equipment held for planned projects or phased deliveries.

Storage Services

Straightforward storage. Additional handling when you need it.

Whether you have a few factory-sealed boxes or a larger batch of equipment, we provide storage with the level of service that suits your needs.

Core Storage

For clients who simply need a reliable local place to keep their equipment.

Receiving

We accept equipment from you, your supplier or a carrier and record the number of packages and the visible condition of their outer packaging.

Storage

Your equipment is stored as received, without opening sealed boxes unless otherwise agreed.

Basic Records

We maintain records of received and released packages.

Release on Request

Your equipment can be released to you or your authorised representative according to agreed instructions.

Optional Handling Services

For clients who need more than storage, additional operations can be arranged:

  • Detailed inventory by item, model or serial number
  • Equipment labelling and photographic documentation
  • Picking, packing and repacking
  • Preparation of individual items or complete equipment kits
  • Dispatch, courier coordination and returns
  • Customised stock and movement reports
